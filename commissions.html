TERMS OF SERVICE

Payment & Process
Payment is done through my Ko-fi donation feature or PayPal. I ask for a 50% Price deposit before I can begin the sketch, with the remaining 50% due after the sketch is delivered. All prices are in USD
Refunds & Usage
Refunds will not be issued once you’ve personally approved the sketch and I’ve started working on the lineart, coloring, or rendering. Minor adjustments are free. These commissions are for personal use only unless commercial rights are discussed. crediting me when posting is greatly aprecciated!
Revisions
I’ll update you on the artwork's progress when necessary, or anytime if you ask. You can request revisions as needed, but please note that changes during the final rendering stage are difficult and not encouraged.
Turnover & Delivery
I aim to deliver the first sketch in less than two weeks, and will probably start focusedly working on it after the sketch is delivered. You’re welcome to check in on the progress anytime! I usually won't take more than 5 weeks to complete a commission!
Social Media
I automatically assume the right to post any artwork I create on social media, If you would like me to not post any artwork let me know beforehand!
