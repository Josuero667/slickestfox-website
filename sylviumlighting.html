What is SylviumLighting?

Hello!! SylviumLighting is my personal Etsy store where I handcraft and sell custom ARGB LED Infinity Pucks! These pucks are designed to give your room, PC case, or display shelves a vibrant, mesmerizing infinity-mirror glow.

The Single Pucks use standard WS2812B data protocols, meaning you will need a separate microcontroller (like an Arduino or ESP32) or a compatible 5V ARGB motherboard header to control them. However, if you are looking for a complete, hassle-free setup, the 3-Pack Bundle comes with a dedicated controller, a power supply, and everything you need for a plug-and-play experience right out of the box!

If you love customizing your own RGB lighting setups and DIY electronics, I highly encourage you to check them out. Every purchase directly supports my art, music, and engineering projects. Thank you so much! X3
